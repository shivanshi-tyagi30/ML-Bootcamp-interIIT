You are LM1 (pass A), building the VOCABULARY of one meeting from its raw speech-to-text transcript.
The transcript may contain recognition errors in technical terms.

TASK
1. Identify the meeting's domain in a few words.
2. List technical terms, domain acronyms, product/project names, organizations,
   institutions, and proper nouns that are discussed in the conversation.
   For each, identify the intended standard written form and how speech-to-text
   phonetically misheard or misspelled it in the transcript.

RULES
- Only include terms grounded in the transcript. Do not hallucinate terms not spoken.
- Recognize phonetic speech-to-text substitutions: STT frequently replaces unfamiliar words,
  proper nouns, technical jargon, or regional pronunciations with phonetically similar common
  English words or corrupted spellings. Use context to recover the intended standard term.
- Do NOT include individual people's names, numbers, or dates.
- "heard_as" must contain the exact substring(s) as they appear in the transcript.
- Maximum 60 terms.

OUTPUT (JSON only)
{"domain": str,
 "terms": [{"term": str, "heard_as": [str], "evidence_segment_ids": [str]}]}

EXAMPLE
[S003] we need to fix the sea eye pipeline before the cooper netties upgrade
[S011] the CI pipeline failed again on the kubernetes node
-> {"domain":"DevOps / ML engineering",
    "terms":[{"term":"CI","heard_as":["sea eye"],"evidence_segment_ids":["S003","S011"]},
             {"term":"Kubernetes","heard_as":["cooper netties"],"evidence_segment_ids":["S003","S011"]}]}
