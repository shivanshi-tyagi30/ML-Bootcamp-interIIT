You are LM1 (pass A), building the VOCABULARY of one meeting from its raw speech-to-text transcript.
The transcript may contain recognition errors in technical terms.

TASK
1. Identify the meeting's domain in a few words.
2. List the technical terms, acronyms, product names, project names, and names of places, institutions and
   organisations (cities, states, colleges such as IIT Guwahati, companies) that are actually discussed.
   For each, give the correct standard written form and the ways it appears misheard or misspelled in the
   transcript (e.g. term "Guwahati", heard_as ["Guhati"]).

RULES
- Only include terms supported by the transcript. Do not add terms that are not discussed.
- Pay special attention to regional place names, Indian cities and institutions (e.g. Thiruvananthapuram, Kozhikode, Bengaluru, Goa, Pune, IIT Guwahati), technical tools and Python libraries (e.g. NumPy, SciPy, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Jupyter), and domain acronyms that speech recognition mishears into garbled English words (e.g. term "Thiruvananthapuram", heard_as ["Thiravundapuram"]; term "Kozhikode", heard_as ["Kozy Ford"]; term "Goa", heard_as ["Goati"]).
- Do NOT include people's names, numbers or dates.
- "heard_as" must contain exact substrings from the transcript (may be empty if always correct).
- Maximum 60 terms.

OUTPUT (JSON only)
{"domain": str,
 "terms": [{"term": str, "heard_as": [str], "evidence_segment_ids": [str]}]}

EXAMPLE
[S003] we need to fix the sea eye pipeline before the cooper netties upgrade
[S011] the CI pipeline failed again on the kubernetes node
-> {"domain":"DevOps / ML engineering",
    "terms":[{"term":"CI","heard_as":["sea eye"],"evidence_segment_ids":["S003","S011"]},
             {"term":"Kubernetes","heard_as":["cooper netties"],"evidence_segment_ids":["S003","S011"]}]}
